# Privacy Technical Audit

Private launch audit for the MINO Consulting KG website. Last reviewed: 19 July 2026.

## Runtime services and data flows

| Service or function | Location | Trigger | Information that may be transmitted | Necessary | Consent before loading | Implementation / remediation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub Pages | Production deployment, `public/CNAME`, `package.json` | Every production page request | IP address, request time, requested path, referrer, browser/device headers and other normal connection data may be processed by the host | Yes, for delivery | No separate opt-in for technically necessary hosting | Custom-domain root deployment is configured. Exact GitHub entity, terms and log retention require legal confirmation. |
| Google Maps embedded iframe | `src/privacy/ConsentControlledMap.jsx`, rendered from `Contact` | Only after the visitor activates the contextual load button or saves Google Maps as enabled | IP address, browser/device data and data generated by interaction with Google Maps may be sent to Google | No | Yes | Iframe is absent by default, mounted only after explicit choice, and immediately unmounted when permission is withdrawn. |
| Google Maps external link | `Contact` in `src/App.jsx` | Only when the visitor follows the link | The destination provider receives ordinary navigation and connection data after the visitor leaves the site | No | No; following the link is a deliberate external navigation, not consent to the embedded map | Kept separate from the load-map action and opened with `noopener noreferrer`. |
| KSW and other legal external links | Legal content and `renderLegalItem` in `src/App.jsx` | Only when the visitor follows a link | Ordinary navigation and connection data at the destination | No | No | Links do not preload external content. New-window links use `noopener noreferrer`. |
| Prepared email (`mailto:`) | `BookingModal`, contact cards and legal pages | Only when the visitor activates an email link | No request is submitted by the website. If the visitor sends the message, the content is processed by the visitor's email provider and the provider serving `office@mino-consulting.at` | No | No separate website consent; sending is controlled by the visitor | Appointment details remain visible. The email provider and retention rules require client confirmation. |
| Telephone links (`tel:`) | Hero, contact cards, legal pages and appointment summary | Only when the visitor activates a telephone link | The browser/device may hand the number to the telephone application or carrier | No | No | Uses verified numbers already present in the repository. |
| Local privacy preference | `src/privacy/preferences.js` | When Google Maps is enabled or privacy settings are saved | No network transmission by this utility | No | The visitor controls it | Versioned key `mino_privacy_preferences_v1`; stores only `{ "googleMaps": true }`. Disabling removes the key. Safe parsing and storage failures are handled. |
| Appointment form browser state | `BookingModal` in `src/App.jsx` | While the visitor uses the open dialog | No network transmission and no browser storage | No | Not applicable | Personal form values stay only in React memory. They are not logged, placed in URLs, or stored in local/session storage. |

## Third-party requests removed

- The Google Fonts stylesheet import was removed from `src/index.css`.
- The Unsplash hero and portrait URLs were removed from `src/App.jsx`.
- The automatically mounted Google Maps iframe was removed from `Contact`.
- Fonts, logo, icons, hero artwork and team placeholder artwork are now served from the same site origin.

## Repository-wide checks

- No analytics, marketing tags, tracking pixels, social widgets or error-reporting SDKs were found.
- No external CSS/JavaScript CDN was found. Hugeicons is installed through npm and bundled into the application.
- No form backend, external API submission, live scheduling integration, CRM integration or uploaded-document portal was found.
- No service worker, browser-language detection, session storage, unique visitor identifier or newsletter function was found.
- `schema.org` URLs in JSON-LD are vocabulary identifiers and do not create browser requests.

## Client confirmation required

- Confirm GitHub Pages is the final production host and review the relevant provider/retention wording.
- Identify the provider serving `office@mino-consulting.at`.
- Confirm retention and deletion practices for appointment inquiries after an email is sent.
- Confirm whether a CRM receives inquiry data outside this website.
- Confirm that analytics, a backend form, a client portal and uploaded-document functions will remain absent at launch.
- Supply and approve the real office/adviser/social images listed in `IMAGE_ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md`.
