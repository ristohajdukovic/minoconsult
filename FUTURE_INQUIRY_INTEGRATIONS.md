# Future Inquiry Integrations

The current website does not transmit inquiry data. It prepares an email in the visitor's own email application, where the visitor reviews and sends it. The following options are documentation only and must not be enabled without customer approval, legal review and an updated privacy assessment.

## Direct form delivery

A future first-party or contractually approved form endpoint could deliver inquiries directly to MINO Consulting KG. Before implementation, confirm the responsible processor, EU/EEA data location, data-processing agreement, retention and deletion periods, transport encryption, spam protection, recipient access, failure handling and the required privacy-notice changes. Preserve an explicit success/failure state and the existing phone and email alternatives. Do not treat a technical delivery confirmation as acceptance of a mandate or appointment.

## Availability-based booking

A future booking service could expose real, maintained availability and create confirmed appointments. Before selecting a provider, obtain customer approval and confirm calendar ownership, supported languages, timezone handling, cancellation and rescheduling rules, reminders, accessibility, data location, processor terms and consent requirements. Do not display simulated availability or describe a requested time as booked until the service returns an actual confirmation.

No provider has been selected. Replacing the current email-preparation mechanism requires a separate scoped decision and implementation task.
