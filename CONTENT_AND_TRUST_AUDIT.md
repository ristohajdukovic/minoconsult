## Cross-site findings

- Positioning was previously adjective-led. It now states services, Vienna location, typical operational problems and the real appointment-request action.
- The strongest defensible proof is company/register/contact data. Ratings, testimonials, client logos, awards, response times and client counts are absent and remain absent.
- “Since 1997” was too ambiguous for marketing. The verified legal statement is that the entity is entered in the register since 14 June 1997; that fact remains in the imprint, not as an experience claim.
- Real client fit, industries, capacity, delivery systems, reporting frequency and commercial terms cannot be finalized without client answers.
- The former industry section implied specializations in property, hospitality and hotels without sufficient proof. It was replaced by typical service situations.
- The appointment path was already technically honest. Its copy now states required information, local preparation through the visitor’s email program and the need for separate confirmation.
- Service pages previously used near-identical three-step marketing structures. They now retain a shared accessible layout but use service-specific inputs, dependencies and limitations.
- Legal and privacy pages remain information pages and were not turned into acquisition pages.

## Terminology and consistency decisions

- German uses Austrian terms including `Buchhaltung`, `Lohnverrechnung`, `Jahresabschluss`, `Finanzamt`, `FinanzOnline` and `Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung (UVA)`.
- Croatian uses `porezno savjetovanje`, `knjigovodstvo`, `obračun plaća`, `porezna prijava` and `poduzeće`; prohibited Serbian ekavian variants are not used.
- Official Austrian names such as Finanzamt, FinanzOnline, KSW, WTBG 2017 and WT-ARL remain untranslated or are explained at first relevant use.
- `Erstgespräch anfragen` / `Zatražite prvi razgovor` denotes a request, never a confirmed booking.

## Remaining trust gaps

See `CLIENT_FACTS_NEEDED.md`. The highest-impact blockers are exact adviser authorization/capacity, actual consultation languages, accepted client groups, onboarding and document workflow, current capacity, pricing, verified opening hours, and approved real photography.
