# AGENTS.md

## Project Scope

This project is a premium editorial landing page for MINO Consulting KG, an accounting and tax consulting business in Vienna.

## Coding And Design Guidelines

- Preserve the current premium editorial style: cream/off-white background, dark green/black accents, thin border system, elegant serif headings, and calm spacing.
- Do not redesign the whole website from scratch. Make scoped, incremental improvements that respect the existing visual system.
- Prioritize conversion, trust, mobile UX, accessibility, and local SEO.
- Do not invent fake reviews, fake ratings, fake client numbers, fake certifications, fake awards, or unverifiable trust claims.
- Where trust data is missing, use neutral editable placeholders or conditional rendering.
- Keep components reusable, readable, and clean.
- Avoid adding large dependencies unless absolutely necessary.
- Do not use generic AI-looking design choices; keep visuals specific, restrained, and professional.
- Do not change existing typography, colors, or spacing tokens unless needed for the task.
- Use semantic HTML, accessible buttons, visible focus states, correct heading order, and responsive image handling.
- After each task, summarize changed files and explain how to test.
